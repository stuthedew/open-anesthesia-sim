Outline:
- 7515: heading "The debt gate".
- 7517-7520: the rule.
  - Recorded technical debt is cleared before a new milestone begins.
  - Phase 0 applied the rule once, to get out of a queue that had stopped shrinking.
  - This section makes the rule standing, so it runs before every milestone.
- 7522-7526: why.
  - Debt is cheapest to clear while someone still remembers the code.
  - A defect carried across a milestone boundary has to be diagnosed again, against code that has moved.
  - A decision left unanswered stops being one that anyone can make.
  - Deferred debt is either paid twice or silently dropped.
- 7528: heading "What counts".
- 7530-7533: what debt is.
  - Debt is what the queue records, minus new work.
  - An open item is debt when it is classed `defect`, `safety`, `science`, `refactor` or `perf`.
  - It is also debt when its status is `needs-decision`, whatever it is about.
- 7535-7537: `feature` and `planning` items are not debt.
  - They are the work the gate protects.
  - Counting them would mean "do everything before doing anything", and no gate could open.
- 7539-7548: process work is debt once the mechanism is live.
  - Building a new tooling capability is new work and does not keep a gate shut.
  - A mechanism that `main` depends on and that is unreliable is debt, classed `defect`. The class carries the rule, so nothing extra is tracked.
  - What decides is the mechanism's state, not its layer. An unbuilt idea costs nothing, while a half-working mechanism costs something every session.
  - Half-finished process machinery must not be in production use: it is either made reliable or abandoned and removed.
- 7550-7555: the same test decides a hazard, the one carve-out from the `safety` and `science` exception.
  - An `anticipated` finding describes a hazard a later milestone will create, so it is not debt until that milestone builds it.
  - Points to § "The gate is a snapshot, not a moving target" for the rule, what it was chosen over, and its cost.
- 7557-7562: caution from the first pass.
  - Classing tooling breakage `infra` rather than `defect` makes it look like a capability nobody has built.
  - `docket release` leaving `uv.lock` stale and breaking `make check` at every release is a defect. Adding release tagging is not.
  - If the class does not separate them, the gate cannot.
- 7564-7566: clearing an item.
  - Clearing means `done` or `dropped`, with the reason recorded.
  - Deciding that something is not worth doing is legitimate, and often right.
  - Leaving an item open and starting anyway is not legitimate.
- 7568: heading "The gate is a snapshot, not a moving target".
- 7570-7575: a freeze closes new scope, not the completeness of a fix (project owner, 2026-08-30).
  - A frozen list exists to fix a specific set of problems, and an entry may need a further finding before it is fixed.
  - Refusing that finding defeats the list's purpose.
  - The rule is deliberately asymmetric: it keeps new work out and lets in what an entry needs.
- 7577-7582: an admitted finding is worked with the entry it completes (project owner, 2026-08-30).
  - Working it later lets the first entry ship half-fixed.
  - Both go in one branch with one review and close together, before anything else starts.
- 7584-7595: three homes for what a frozen entry waits on (`PL-D388`, project owner, 2026-10-03, ratified, over admitting every open prerequisite of an entry automatically).
  - First: work named in the milestone's `Required scope` makes the entry the milestone's, per § "Debt inside the milestone's own scope".
  - Second: work that no release schedules before the milestone begins defers the entry on the terms of § "The cadence" step 3, recorded as `deferred-from:`.
  - Third: anything else is let in beside the entry, dated, and worked with it.
  - `bin/docket check` rejects an open frozen entry waiting on work that none of the three covers, when the release train does not schedule that work first (`PL-8QMD`).
- 7597-7605: the list freezes when the gate freezes.
  - The moment is the later of the milestone's scoping and the ship of the milestone before it (§ "The cadence").
  - A finding re-enters this gate when its problem was present at that moment, whatever its id and however late someone noticed it.
  - A finding defers to the next gate only when its problem is new: introduced while clearing this gate, or while implementing the milestone it protects.
- 7607-7617: why the test is presence, not discovery.
  - Work generates findings: clearing 13 items in one session generated 14 new ones, which is normal and is the capture rule doing its job.
  - If a gate reopened for everything noticed after the freeze, its queue would never empty and its milestone would never start.
  - The rule would then be abandoned, which is worse than not having it.
  - The test still closes one gap: a problem found again under a new id, after a stranded branch or a slow session, re-enters (`PL-64LS`).
- 7619-7626: friction that compounds is the clearest case of a problem present at the freeze (project owner, 2026-08-30).
  - Such a finding costs something again on every remaining entry. Examples: a tool that answers the wrong question, a check that cries wolf, a command that ends red when it succeeds.
  - It was almost always present at the freeze, and only became visible once the gate's work started paying its cost.
  - Deferring it means paying that cost once per remaining entry, so it re-enters and is worked early.
- 7628-7636: the test is arithmetic.
  - Multiply what each remaining entry pays by the number of entries left.
  - `PL-0RS6` qualified at 18 entries, each paying a twelve-command detour. That is a large fraction of the saving the release exists to deliver.
  - Work that is merely valuable, cleaner or more interesting fails the test and waits for the roadmap, for example rewriting a working tool in another language or adopting a nicer abstraction.
  - If nobody can name the saving per entry, the finding does not qualify.
- 7638-7649: presence is a presumption, and sessions should favour it.
  - A finding that continues or completes a frozen item belongs to this gate and is recorded with that item. `PL-SWFM`, which continues `PL-Z4GF`, motivated this.
  - A session may still defer a finding that qualifies when it has no real link to anything frozen, or when pulling it in would recreate the refilling-queue problem that the debt gate replaced Phase 0 to solve.
  - A session may also decline a finding that only superficially resembles frozen scope.
  - Either way it must say so and why. Silently changing which gate a finding belongs to is the renegotiation the freeze prevents.

- 7885-7905: record a deferral on the item.

  - The version is the milestone whose list excuses the item, never the gate it goes to, because the next freeze takes every open debt item. The reason states the grounds.
