Closes `PL-CG7J` (per-trace show/hide controls on the compartment chart).

## What changed

The six compartment traces were drawn unconditionally, with no way to look at
one. "Why does fat lag muscle" is a two-trace question and it is unreadable
against four other lines crossing it, which is why Gas Man's graph pane carries
a checkbox per trace.

**The legend is the control.** Each entry is now a checkbox labelled with the
compartment, rather than a legend beside a separate row of six boxes. Two lists
of the same six compartments is exactly how a legend comes to name a line the
chart is not drawing — they agree only while somebody keeps them agreeing.
Here the entry *is* the state: box, swatch and label are written in one place
from the same flag the chart's own series list is built from. A hidden entry
shows an unchecked box, an empty swatch and a muted label, and keeps its width,
because a row that reflowed under the cursor would move the next box out from
under it.

**One table instead of three.** A trace was previously described in three
places — where the series was built, in the `PlottedSeries` pairing table, and
in a hand-written legend row six hundred lines away. It is now one
`_CompartmentTrace` record carrying the quantity it draws, how it is drawn, the
legend entry that names it, and whether it is shown. The `(series, quantity)`
pair is manufactured from that record, so filtering the table — which is how a
hidden trace is left out of a frame — cannot misalign the pairing the way
filtering two parallel sequences could.

**Hidden means removed, not blanked.** A hidden trace leaves the chart's
`data_series` rather than being left in place with an empty point list, which is
what stops the client holding its points at all.

## Why it is safe

Hiding a trace is presentation state only: it reaches no model state, changes no
recorded sample, and leaves identical inputs producing identical results. Every
compartment's concentration stays in the numeric readouts whatever is drawn, so
`docs/MODEL.md`'s minimum displayed outputs are met by the readouts and never by
the chart — a plot showing two curves is a chosen view of six modelled
compartments, and the panel says so. When every compartment is unchecked the
chart says that too, because a blank panel reads as a display that has failed.

A trace left out of `redraw_visible_window` is *not* blanked — it keeps the
points of the frame it was last drawn in. That is what makes hiding one free,
and it is why it must also come off the chart; the contract is now written into
`chart_series.redraw_visible_window`, and a trace being shown again is redrawn
*before* it is put back on the chart, so a stale curve never reaches a reader.

## Tests

Eight unit tests and two integration tests, all new:

- the pairing survives the filter — hidden traces removed, the four still shown
  each verified against their own compartment's recorded samples;
- a hidden trace is not drawn rather than drawn empty, and the drawing order
  (marks, then references, then traces) survives the rebuild;
- a hidden trace stops being redrawn while the run advances, and is current
  again the moment it is shown;
- the legend's three channels agree with the chart's own series list;
- the chart says so when no compartment is drawn;
- hiding one leaves every other trace's points and all six readouts untouched;
- **on a live Flet session**: hiding a trace sends the client one `Remove` per
  series — the half a unit test cannot reach — and a frame with two traces drawn
  sends nine fewer patch operations than one with six, measured on the recording
  connection rather than argued.

Every test drives the checkbox's own `on_change`, so a closure bound to the
wrong trace fails rather than passing quietly.
