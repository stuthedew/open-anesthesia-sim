## The defect

`bin/docket new` records a re-filing onto the item it matched. At the generator floor — two recurrences, which is three filings — the session-start digest and `docket next` both name the cluster so a reader can open the briefs and write `root-cause-of:` by hand.

That field moves a queue position and does nothing else. `plan.recommend` builds its generator map from the startable set, so a claim written onto an id that set excludes is read by nothing. `plan.recurring` filtered on `CLOSED_STATUSES` alone, which is half the population `recommend` will not rank — so the line could ask a reader to confirm a promotion that could not act.

Two recorded instances, which is what made it a mechanism rather than an accident:

- `PL-W7WL` was ratified onto the generator tier on 2026-09-21 while it was being implemented. The write would have been a no-op, onto front matter its own close-out was about to rewrite.
- `PL-GYRX` was dropped on 2026-09-19 reasoning identically about `impairs-generators:`, the sibling entrance to the same tier.

## The change

`plan.recurring` takes the in-flight ids `recommend` already excludes on, and both call sites pass the ids they were already holding — `_say_recurring` from `cmd_next`'s flight report, `format_digest` from its own.

**Surfacing at `docket next` was already there.** The item's own recommendation was to report the candidate where a reader can act on it; `PL-X5JR` had already landed that half. The moment was right and the population was wrong, so this is the filter rather than a new surface.

**`untriaged` and `blocked` are deliberately kept, though `_startable` excludes them too.** Their window has not opened rather than closed: a claim written onto an untriaged item ranks the moment triage seats it, and is what a triage pass most wants before choosing a band; one on a blocked item ranks when its blocker clears. Dropping them would have traded this defect for the same defect pointing the other way. Measured before deciding — 4 items sat at or above the floor, 2 open and named, and neither was untriaged or blocked, so the carve-out cost nothing.

**A third surface asserted the old rule out loud.** `_record_recurrence` printed "`bin/docket next` now names it as a generator-tier promotion candidate" on the filing that crossed the floor — a prediction about another command's output, which the filter made false for a matched item already on a branch. It now states the floor, a fact about the count, and carries the condition rather than resolving it: `docket new` reads no refs, and buying an accurate sentence with a git call would put that cost on the capture path `CLAUDE.md` keeps cheap. `docket show` is deliberately unchanged — its "That is the generator threshold" is a fact about the count too, and it prints the `IN FLIGHT` mark two lines above.

**What it cannot catch, stated rather than implied.** An unpushed branch is in no ref, which is the state `PL-W7WL` was actually in. The filter catches every pushed in-flight branch and no unpushed one; the docstring says so, and `FlightReport.unreadable` with `_say_unread` already carry that gap beside the answer.
