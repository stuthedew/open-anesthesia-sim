## `docs/MODEL.md`

A new top-level § "What a branch comparison asserts, and what it does not", placed immediately before § "Interface boundary": the last thing said about the model before the document turns to what may be displayed. Four parts.

**What is compared, and what the two runs share.** A trunk and one branch of it, never N — a second fork is refused rather than replacing the one on screen, and no branch is read against another. Then the eight inherited things, each naming the test that holds it: the patient, the agent (and both locks), the equations and model version, the circuit volume, the step, every control value up to the fork, the bookmarks, and the time frame. What differs is the control timeline after the fork and nothing else, so the attribution is the complement of a checkable list rather than an assurance.

**The shared history is identical by construction, not by measurement** — and two things that does *not* buy. It bounds nothing past the fork: how far the two runs diverge, in which direction, and whether they converge again are properties of the settings, and no construction property limits them. And it is a statement about the canonical path rather than the one the chart draws — for a bookmark fork the drawn columns agree only to floating-point composition, which is why no difference trace is drawn.

**Neither run is a prediction for a patient.** One reference adult, no covariate by which one patient is distinguished from another, nothing scaling a volume, flow or coefficient from one. "Low flow woke this patient twelve minutes sooner" is refused clause by clause: no patient, no measure of responsiveness (no effect-site compartment, F_a is defined equal to F_A), and no claim to minutes-precise emergence. A branch dialled to a different cardiac output is shown not to be a second patient either — a real patient differing in cardiac output differs in compartment volumes too, and this model holds those fixed, so moving one half of the coupled pair shifts every tissue time constant in a direction the intact pair need not take.

**What the interface may and may not assert**, with the line drawn where two shipped surfaces sit close to it and neither crosses: a bookmark halt reads an instant a run *reached* rather than one predicted for an endpoint it has not, and a control-change record states its own run's inputs rather than the difference between two.
