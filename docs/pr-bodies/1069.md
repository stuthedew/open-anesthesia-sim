**Before:** `tools/generator_check.py` read the ids a commit subject leads with through a regular expression of its own. It was case-sensitive, took only commas between ids, and required a colon after them. `docket` answers the same question with `vcs.leading_ids`, which takes any case, a comma, `&` or `and`, and no colon.
