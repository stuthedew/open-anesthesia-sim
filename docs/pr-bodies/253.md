`core/agent_simulation_validation.py` halts a run whose agent accounting has drifted, and three constants decide how far it has to drift first:

```text
AGENT_ACCOUNTING_ABSOLUTE_TOLERANCE_L = 1e-12
AGENT_ACCOUNTING_RELATIVE_TOLERANCE  = 1e-9
MINIMUM_RELATIVE_SCALE_L             = 1e-15
```

Nothing in `tests/`, `tools/` or `docs/` named any of them, so each could be loosened by orders of magnitude with the whole suite green — a documented release tolerance that no gate held. At 1e-3 relative, an eight-hour case at the envelope corner (384 L delivered) would carry 0.38 L unaccounted, about a fifth of the circuit and alveolar store, without the guard firing.

Nothing shipped was wrong; the margin simply rested on nobody having edited a constant.

## What is here

Eight tests in `tests/unit/test_agent_simulation_validation.py`. **Nothing under `src/` changed** — the guard and its constants are correct, and what was missing is anything that would notice them changing.

- **One pinning test**, `test_the_accounting_tolerances_are_the_documented_release_tolerances`, asserting the three against the values `docs/MODEL.md` § "Mass-balance identity" documents. Its failure message names the section a deliberate change has to revise. This is the idiom `tests/reference/test_coupled_dynamics.py` already uses twice, for the supported input ranges and for the interface's displayed resolution.
- **Four cases 10% either side of the two thresholds.** Each asserts that the other branch of the abs-OR-rel pass condition is out of reach, so the case probes one threshold rather than the disjunction, and the helper that builds each residual asserts that the residual it constructed is the one asked for — a case that landed on the wrong side of a threshold would pass or fail for a reason unrelated to the guard and go on looking correct.
- **Two cases at the thresholds exactly.** `docs/MODEL.md` § "Mass-balance test" states the condition inclusively (|ε| ≤ ε_max), and tightening either comparison to `<` moves the boundary by one float ulp, which the ±10% cases cannot see. Both are constructed so the residual is exact rather than rounded: a picolitre delivered against an empty store for the absolute branch, and a litre delivered against a litre exhausted with a nanolitre left over for the relative one. Building the second the obvious way — storing a litre less a nanolitre — would round the residual by ~5e-17 and settle the case on whichever side of the threshold that rounding happened to fall.
- **One case holding `MINIMUM_RELATIVE_SCALE_L`** to being the denominator before anything has been delivered. The floor decides no verdict and cannot — at 1e-15 L the relative branch admits only a residual at or below 1e-24 L, which the absolute branch already admits — so what is pinned is that the reported relative error stays finite and is the residual over the floor.

The restated values sit in module constants that the boundary cases use in place of the imported ones. A case measured against the constant it is testing would follow that constant wherever it moved, which is the defect itself; the pinning test is the only place the two are compared.
