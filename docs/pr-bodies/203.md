## Why the name mattered

"Respiratory system" clinically means the patient's own lungs and airways, which maps only to `alveoli`. The class also owned `circuit` — anesthesia-machine equipment — and `PatientCompartments`, holding the vessel-rich, muscle and fat tissue groups. That is a safety concern rather than a tidiness one: `total_stored_agent_l` sums all of them and feeds the conservation check that can halt a run, and at steady state most of the agent is in fat and muscle. A reader who took the name at face value read that quantity as agent in the lungs, and was wrong by a large factor on an accounting quantity.

## The four parts

**1 — the rename.** `core/respiratory_system.py` → `core/uptake_system.py`; `RespiratorySystem` → `AgentUptakeSystem`; `RespiratoryStepResult` → `UptakeStepResult`; `SimulationState.respiratory_system` → `.uptake_system`. "Uptake" is the canonical term in inhaled-anesthetic pharmacology for agent moving from circuit through alveoli into tissue. The module docstring now carries the reason, replacing a pointer to a `docs/WORKING_NOTES.md` thread that no longer exists — a dangling citation `doc_check` cannot currently see, because it reads neither source docstrings nor section names (`PL-X2XX`).

**2 — the setter facade, kept and defined.** The class docstring states what makes the four a set: the live user-controllable inputs, machine controls and patient state alike, and exactly the sliders the interface exposes. It also states what does *not* belong there, using circuit volume as the worked example, so the set has a boundary rather than being whatever accumulated.

**3 — conservation moved into `core/`.** `BreathingCircuit.set_circuit_volume` now conserves the agent in the circuit and refuses a volume too small to hold it, checking **before** it mutates so a refused volume leaves the circuit untouched. The brief's measurement reproduced exactly against the shipped code: 0.048288200 L in the circuit after 60 s, halved to 0.024144100 L by the setter — 24.1 mL of equivalent agent gas destroyed — with the next `advance(0.1)` raising `AgentSimulationValidationError`, blaming the numerics for a setter's defect.

`SimulationController.set_circuit_volume` is now three lines of forwarding, and the controller **no longer imports `SimulationConfigurationError` at all** — it had been raising a core exception from outside core, which is the same boundary defect stated the other way round.

**4 — the second facade dropped.** `SimulationState.circuit`, `.alveoli` and `.patient` re-exported attributes under shorter names, giving every compartment two reachable paths. Callers reach `uptake_system.circuit`; three imports left `core/simulation.py` with them.
