Closes `PL-B9PY` (decompose `SimulationView` so two runs can be rendered at once), the ninth of v0.5.0's Required-scope entries and stage 3 of `PL-WB0X` (split `simulation_view.py`).

## What changed

`SimulationView` was one class holding one run's widgets and one `SimulationController` reference. It is now two, split along the line `ROADMAP.md` § "v0.5.0 — the case you can branch" already drew and `PL-8PSW`/`PL-HLD5` settled the encoding for:

- **`RunView`** holds everything belonging to a particular run — its controller, its six readouts and their MAC lines, its four settings, its transport and playback rate, the agent it is on, its record of what was changed during it, its notices, and the lines it draws. It is instantiable more than once and reaches its host through exactly two callbacks (`refresh_dashboard`, `defer_render`) and nothing else, which is what makes "two of these against two controllers" a property of the code rather than a hope.
- **`SimulationView`** holds what two runs must share: the compartment chart and the wash-in chart, their axes, the window both are drawn in, the time base that chooses it, the clinical references ruled across it, and the compartment selection. It takes a sequence of controllers and builds one `RunView` per run.

`_CompartmentTrace` became the compartment rather than the line — identity, colour, dash pattern, legend entry and visibility are shared across runs, and each run builds its own `fch.LineChartData` from them via `build_line()`. That is the split `PL-HLD5` implies: across a comparison the compartment keeps colour *and* line style, and the run is to be carried on line width.

The compartment is the outer loop of the drawing order and the run the inner one, so the two lines a reader has to tell apart are adjacent — which is what the run-on-line-width encoding rests on, and is now asserted rather than assumed.

## Three refusals rather than three surprises

- Runs on different agents are refused at construction: one ×MAC ruler, one MAC-awake band and one 1 MAC line are all the agent's own published values, and two agents on one axis would leave one run's traces read against the other's divisor.
- `MAX_DISPLAYED_RUNS = 2` holds the milestone's own out-of-scope bound, "never more than two runs displayed at once". At three, colour and line style carry the compartment and line width is a two-level channel, so a third curve would be drawn with nothing identifying it.
- A dashboard with no run is refused, having no agent and nothing to rule an axis from.

A raise out of the render loop now halts *every* run, because one frame is drawn for all of them and the raise is attributable to none; a raise out of one run's step or setter still halts only that run.

## What this deliberately does not do

Two runs render; nothing yet says *which* run a curve belongs to. That encoding is `PL-8PSW`'s (overlay two branches on one time axis), and inventing a different one here would have decided it in the wrong place. It is recorded in `SimulationView`'s own docstring, and `main.py` builds one run.

Both classes stayed in `simulation_view.py` on purpose. `tools/contrast_check.py` and `tools/agent_identity_check.py` both hard-code that path, and the second matches `_apply_agent_color_scheme` by name inside it, so giving the per-run class a module of its own would have left the accessibility check reporting a pass over an empty identity set. `PL-V53R` is that finding; the module boundary is `PL-25KS`'s to draw, since the Qt port builds the view decomposed from the start.
