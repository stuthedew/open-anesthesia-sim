One pair of constants in `core/supported_ranges.py` stood for two different claims about the fresh gas flow:

| Claim | What it says | Now declared in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The model's envelope | the range the compartment structure is claimed to represent a patient over | `MINIMUM_FRESH_GAS_FLOW_L_MIN` / `MAXIMUM_FRESH_GAS_FLOW_L_MIN`, unchanged |
| A machine's deliverable range | what that machine's flowmeters and minimum-flow floor can physically set | `deliverable_fresh_gas_flow_range` on the machine profile |

Merged, a second profile whose flowmeter reaches 15 L/min would widen the domain every reference gate was driven over with no statement in that module changing — an exact number produced outside everything verified, and indistinguishable on screen from one inside it. The converse fails the same way: a machine floored at 0.5 L/min would either be unrepresentable or would move the model's floor for every other machine.

## What changed

- **`core/circuit.py`** gains `DeliverableFreshGasFlowRange` (frozen, refusing a negative, non-finite or inverted range) and `BreathingCircuit.deliverable_fresh_gas_flow_range`, defaulting to `None`. `_require_deliverable_flow` refuses a flow outside it in the machine's own words, saying in the same sentence that the model's envelope is not what refused it; `_require_the_two_flow_claims_overlap` refuses, at construction, a profile that overlaps the envelope nowhere, rather than leaving it to be discovered one refused setting at a time.
- **`core/supported_ranges.py`** keeps its numbers unchanged and now states that the interval is the model's and only the model's.
- **`core/parameters.py`** carries the field through `_BreathingCircuitPayload` and `BreathingCircuitParameters`; **`core/uptake_system.py`** passes it to the circuit in `for_agent()`.
- **`data/machines/reference_circle_system.json`** declares `"deliverable_fresh_gas_flow_range": null`, with its `provenance_gap` naming survey section (b8) as the authority for the unknown.
- **`docs/MODEL.md`** § "Supported input ranges" gains "The model's envelope and a machine's deliverable range are two claims".

## Nothing displayed or refused changes today

No operator's manual or manufacturer specification giving a deliverable range was reachable for any of the eight machines the 2026-09-19 survey covered, so the shipped profile records the unknown. A `null` is an **absence of claim and not a machine known to be unlimited**: the envelope alone binds, exactly as before the field existed. What the work buys is that the second machine cannot silently widen it.

## Two things deliberately not built

`minimum_total_flow_l_min` and `minimum_oxygen_flow_l_min`, which `docs/machine-abstraction.md` names beside the range, are left out on that document's own admission rule. A machine's minimum total fresh gas flow **is** the range's floor, and two names for one number in a safety-critical data file is the conflation this item removes; a minimum oxygen flow reaches nothing a model carrying one volatile agent and no oxygen computes.
