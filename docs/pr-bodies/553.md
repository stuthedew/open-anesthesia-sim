## What landed

`docs/MODEL.md` gains **"The chart's hover readout: what the tooltip may show"**, deriving the contents as § "Displayed precision" derives the numeric readouts':

- **Three lines** — run context, then the compartment with its gloss, then the value in both units — with the qualifiers ahead of the number, because a hedge placed below the value it hedges is a warning rather than a prevention.
- **Every number through `app/formatting.py`**, never the chart library's own formatter, with a measured table of what `.3g` prints instead. At 48.3 s the fat compartment reads `3.52e-05` against the readout's `<0.01%`: five decimal places past the derived resolution, with no unit, on a chart carrying two axes that differ by a factor of two for sevoflurane.
- **The readouts' own resolution**, with the argument for a finer hover readout answered rather than inherited. The hover is the display most exposed to being read as a patient-facing value, so it is the last one that should carry a digit only the within-run reading supports.
- **Which series answer a hover**, and why the clinical references and control marks do not — an interaction reason, since all three cross every trace.
- **That it answers running or paused.** The paused-only restriction was a property of Flet's per-frame per-point diff, not of the affordance.
