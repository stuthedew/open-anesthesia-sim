Closes **PL-LKCN** (pin PL-010's no-rebuild property with an allocation test on the render tick).

## What

`tests/unit/test_simulation_view.py` gains `test_refresh_allocates_no_chart_points_when_drawn_count_unchanged`, which counts `fch.LineChartDataPoint` construction across sixty render ticks at a saturated 900 s window.

PL-010 (stop rebuilding render objects on every frame) was justified by a wall-clock figure — 16.6 ms to 2.6 ms — measured with a harness written in a scratch directory and thrown away with the session. Nothing in the repository could re-measure it. What regressed was allocation rather than milliseconds, and allocation is deterministic, so this runs in the ordinary suite instead of flaking on a shared runner the way a timing ceiling would.

The timing harness is deliberately **not** built, per the triage decision recorded on the item.

## Why it is a conditional

The drawn count is not fixed frame to frame even at a saturated window: M4 contributes up to four points per column and two on the monotone stretches, so a sliding window crosses column boundaries where the total moves by a point or two per trace. Measured over the sixty frames: 278 points per trace, dropping to 276 and back as boundaries pass. Frames that grow a trace allocate the difference, correctly. The ~50 frames that draw an unchanged count allocate nothing, and those are the ones asserted on.

Two guards against a vacuous pass are asserted: that such frames occurred at all, and that every frame's drawn coordinates moved. The latter is the trap the item names — Flet's `Prop.__set__` early-returns on an equal value, so a replayed fixed snapshot exercises a cheaper write than the running app performs, and would also pin the drawn count.

## How it was validated

Both regressions were injected and confirmed to fail it:

- the pre-PL-010 rebuild (`redraw_points` discarding its points and building the frame fresh) — fails on frame 1, 1946 points built while drawing the same 1946;
- allocating a throwaway point per drawn sample while preserving the identity of every point the series keeps — which `test_chart_points_are_moved_rather_than_rebuilt_each_frame` **passes**, since it compares surviving references where this one counts construction.

No source changed; `src/` is untouched.
