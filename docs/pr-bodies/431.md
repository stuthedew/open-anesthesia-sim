## The change

`ORACLE_STEP_S` 0.05 s → 0.0125 s, `_oracle_step_for` `shipped / 2` → `shipped / 8`, `EXACT_STEP_ORACLE_TOLERANCE` `5e-12` → `4e-13`. The two step constants must move together or the lockstep test fails; `0.1 / 8.0` is exactly `0.0125` because division by a power of two is exact in binary floating point.

0.0125 s is the knee rather than a round number. The gate's worst figure falls 7.7377e-13, 4.7351e-14, 1.3906e-14, 1.2490e-14 at oracle steps 0.05, 0.025, 0.0125 and 0.00625 s, and stops falling at 0.0125 s because there is nothing left of the oracle's truncation to remove. 0.025 s was measured and rejected — the oracle still contributes about two thirds of the number there.

## The tolerance is set by platform variation, not by the residual

This is the part the brief did not anticipate. Solving the gate trajectories at 0.1, 0.05 and 0.025 s with no oracle involved — the same exact propagator, differently subdivided, so every difference is the shipped side's own accumulation — moves the answer by up to **1.0691e-13**. That is *larger* than the **5.8870e-14** worst residual it accompanies, so the spread and not the measurement sets the floor.

`4e-13` clears the spread by 3.7× and the residual by 6.8×, against the 3.2× `HELD_RUN_ROUNDING_BOUND` already clears its own spread by. The two bounds are now sized by the same rule.

## Sensitivity, measured rather than asserted

Scaling every shipped state by (1 + 1e-11) — what a solver that had stopped being exact would produce — takes the worst gate figure to 1.8084e-12. The old `5e-12` passes that silently; the new bound fails it. A 2e-12 relative degradation still passes, and the floor above is why. **Refining the oracle alone, with the tolerance left at `5e-12`, would have bought none of this** — the tightening is where the sensitivity arrives.

## What the refinement revealed about the trajectories

With the oracle converged the five gate trajectories no longer separate: at the shipped step they sit within a factor of 1.8 (1.5786e-14 down to 8.7985e-15), because what remains is rounding and rounding is set by step count, which is 6 000 for all of them. The envelope corner and the ventilator start now earn their place by the domain they cover rather than by the size of the residual they produce, and the docstrings say so instead of implying otherwise.

Eight of the nine step-ladder parametrizations are now worst at the *finest* step, as rounding predicts. The ninth runs the other way by 1.7×, which is why `test_the_disagreement_does_not_shrink_with_the_step` still asserts no direction.
