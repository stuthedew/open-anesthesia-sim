## PL-H4N8 — item 28 specified agent cost from the exhausted amount

`core/agent_simulation_validation.py` holds the accounting identity `initial + delivered = exhausted + currently stored`. With `initial` at zero, delivered **is** the total and the exhausted/stored pair says only where the agent is at that instant. Cost is what left the bottle, which is what the vaporizer added: `delivered_agent_l`. `exhausted_agent_l` is what has left the *circuit*, and understates delivered by exactly `currently_stored_agent_l`.

It reads plausibly because the two converge at full washout — this model has no metabolism and no chemical degradation. They are not equal at any earlier instant, and the gap is worst during wash-in, which is the phase the low-flow lesson is about.

### Measured, 2026-09-19

`AgentUptakeSystem.default()` — reference adult, sevoflurane held at a fixed 1 MAC dial, 0.1 s steps, `agent_simulation_validation` asserted to pass at every sample. The percentage by which exhausted sits below delivered:

| fresh gas flow | 15 min | 30 min | 60 min |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1.0 L/min | 63.7% low | 53.8% low | 45.7% low |
| 4.0 L/min (shipped default) | 28.6% low | 21.4% low | 16.9% low |
| 8.0 L/min | 16.2% low | 11.8% low | 9.1% low |

The single-run gap understates the problem, because the error is worst at **low** flow and therefore distorts the comparison the lesson *is* rather than shifting both arms together. At a fixed dial delivered is proportional to flow, so 1 against 8 L/min is exactly **8:1** at all three times. On the exhausted basis the same pair reads **18.4:1**, **15.3:1** and **13.4:1** — overstating the low-flow saving by as much as **2.3x**, in the direction of flattering its own lesson.

The item carries the full delivered/exhausted/stored table, the method, and the caveat that a fixed dial is not the equal-alveolar-concentration comparison a real low-flow protocol makes.
