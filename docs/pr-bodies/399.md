The item was built on reading Mapleson 1973's quantifying tables. That reading is unreachable from a session: all three Mapleson papers resolve in PubMed as metadata only (no abstract for any of the three, no PMC record), and `WebFetch` is refused for `doi.org`, `pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov`, `pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov`, `europepmc.org`, `journals.physiology.org`, `sciencedirect.com`, `bjanaesthesia.org.uk` and `gasmanweb.com`. ## The finding that reaches a displayed curve

Fat is perfused about twice as fast as the reachable resting measurement. Time constant is `V·λ/Q`, so that is a factor of two on the fat group's time constant, and therefore on how much agent fat has taken up by the end of a case and on the slow tail of washout. Recorded in `docs/MODEL.md` § "Known limitations" as well as in the data file. Nothing was changed — one depot in six subjects does not overturn a whole-body lumped compartment, and `data/` is protected.
