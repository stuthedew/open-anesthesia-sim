## The entry

Under "The documents", between Jugel and Philip, in the shape `### Baker & Farmery 2011` already uses: **Not held here** with the reason, the citation with ISBN and OCLC rather than a DOI a 1981 monograph does not have, and what the document establishes.

It carries the one thing only it can: that this document was reached by **interlibrary loan**, with both instalments' page ranges, dates, lenders and item ids — and that the pages outside those two ranges were not supplied and are not read.

Four findings are **summarised with the data file named as the record**, not reproduced. An entry restating the whole `sources[1]` note would be a second copy to keep true.

- **The model patient is 100 kg, not 70.** Figure 4.1b and every chapter-5 table are tabulated for 100 kg, which page 56 says is so organ weights read as per cent of body weight — so the material reaches this project as fractions, and every 70-kg figure from it is this project's arithmetic rather than the book's.
- **The book cites its figures onward and measures nothing here.** Page 56 attributes figure 4.1b to four references, all four since identified and read (`PL-8SDL`, `PL-LT51`). Three are uptake-and-distribution models, which consume organ volumes rather than measure them, and two of those are the same document one link apart. The tier is 2 and cannot become 1.
- **The vessel-rich pair reproduces exactly and uniquely; muscle and fat reproduce under no grouping at all.** Kidney + heart + brain + liver gives 6.02 L and 76% of cardiac output at 70 kg against the stored 6.0 and 0.76, and an exhaustive search of all 1,023 groupings of the book's ten compartments finds no other match — and zero matches for muscle, zero for fat.
- **The cardiac-output relation is derived, not measured.** Page 59's `0.2 × M^(3/4)` is built in chapter 2 from Brody's interspecies metabolic allometry divided by an arteriovenous oxygen difference assumed constant across mammals. **4.84 L/min at 70 kg is not a measured human cardiac output**, which is why `docs/MODEL.md` § "Known limitations" says so wherever it stands beside a measured value.
