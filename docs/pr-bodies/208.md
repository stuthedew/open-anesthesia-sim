## The decision the item asked for

PL-007 left one question open — is the module docstring enough, or does each `_...Payload` need a local marker? Its own "Done when" settles it: *a reader landing on either `_...Payload` class can tell why it exists **without scrolling to the module docstring***. So the module docstring is not sufficient.

The rationale went on **`_StrictPayload`**, which every payload names on its own class line — one hop from wherever a reader lands, rather than 150 lines up. Each `_...Payload` then carries a one-line docstring saying what it loads and what it becomes, and the two public dataclasses carry one pointing back, because the temptation to collapse a pair starts at whichever end you meet first.

## The explanation turned out stronger than the brief assumed

The brief calls the split "confusing duplication". I checked that before writing it down, and **it is not duplication at all**:

| | payload | public type |
|---|---|---|
| agent | `_AgentPayload` — 8 fields | `AgentParameters` — 10 fields |
| patient | `_ReferenceAdultPayload` — 10 fields | `ReferenceAdultParameters` — 15 fields |

A payload mirrors the *file's* nesting — three coefficients under `tissue_gas_partition_coefficients`, six values under `tissue_groups` — while the public type is flat and adds the tissue:blood ratios it derives. The seam functions are that mapping, and the fields that look copied are the subset where the two shapes happen to agree.

That reframes the pair from "duplication with a justification" to "two different shapes with a translation between them", which is both true and much harder to talk yourself into deleting. `docs/MODEL.md:766` independently corroborates the derived-ratios half.

The other reason is the boundary: a payload is a load-time artifact, discarded by `parse_*_parameters()`, so nothing else in `core/` imports Pydantic.

## Held by a check, not by hope

`test_every_payload_model_documents_why_it_exists` walks `_StrictPayload.__subclasses__()` — the same tree the strictness test walks, for the same reason — and fails on a payload model with no docstring. Confirmed to fail by stripping one.

It deliberately does **not** check what a docstring says: that is the judgment half, and a check guessing at it would be worse than none. Three payload models (`_TissueGasPartitionCoefficientsPayload`, `_TissueGroupPayload`, `_TissueGroupsPayload`) had no docstring at all before this; a seventh added later now arrives explained or fails `make check`.
