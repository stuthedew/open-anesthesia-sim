Closes the gate entry `PL-9Y42` (validate wash-in against a published human measurement).

## What this adds

Nothing in this repository had ever been compared to a measurement taken outside it. Every test in `tests/reference/` checked the implementation against itself — an analytic exponential, an RK4 oracle of the project's own equations, mass balance, equilibrium, the zero-flow limits, directional ordering. All of that is **verification**: the equations are solved correctly. None of it is **validation**: that the equations describe the phenomenon.

`tests/reference/test_published_wash_in.py` closes that gap against Yasuda et al.'s two 1991 volunteer studies, which report `F_A/F_I` at 30 minutes for all three shipped agents. The tolerance is each cohort's own published standard deviation, not a fitted value.

| Agent | Cohort | Model | Published | Distance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sevoflurane | n=7 | 0.8528 | 0.850 ± 0.018 | +0.15 SD |
| Isoflurane | n=7 | 0.7413 | 0.733 ± 0.027 | +0.31 SD |
| Desflurane | n=8 | 0.9079 | 0.900 ± 0.010 | +0.79 SD |
| Isoflurane | n=8 | 0.7413 | 0.730 ± 0.030 | +0.38 SD |

Isoflurane is compared against both cohorts rather than one, so an agent matched in one study and missed in the other would show up.

## Why the comparison is not circular

Pinning three numbers proves little on its own; four further assertions establish that the operating point is not doing the work:

- the operating point is read from `reference_adult.json` at run time, so alveolar ventilation and cardiac output cannot be tuned to make the comparison pass;
- `F_A/F_I` is independent of the delivered fraction, which is why one dial setting may stand for the four different inspired concentrations the two protocols used (0.4%, 0.6%, 1.0%, 2.0%);
- the agreement survives every fresh gas flow from 1 to 10 L/min, so the one setting the test does choose is not carrying the result;
- the agreement is measurably sensitive to both patient flows, so a reader can see how specific the operating point is.

## Two additions to the item's brief

Both came from measurements taken while building it, and both are recorded in the code and in `docs/MODEL.md`:

- **Cardiac output is as sensitive an axis as ventilation.** The brief named only ventilation. A reader given one axis would under-read how specific the operating point is, so both are documented — and the claims are asserted separately, because they are not equally strong: 1 L/min of ventilation either way puts all three agents outside their published spread, while 1 L/min of cardiac output moves every agent by more than a published SD without leaving the band in both directions.
- **The gate's discriminating power is measured rather than assumed.** Perturbing each agent's blood:gas coefficient until the comparison fails shows the reference point alone tolerates roughly a fifth for two of three agents, which the flow sweep roughly halves. That makes this a coarse gate — it excludes a structurally wrong model, not a mis-parameterized one — and both the module and `docs/MODEL.md` say so rather than letting a pass be over-read.
