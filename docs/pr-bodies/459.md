Closes `PL-CXYT` (docs/MODEL.md does not say whether fresh gas flow includes the vapor the vaporizer adds), a Gate 1 P1 entry on the frozen v0.5.0 debt gate.

## The gap

`docs/MODEL.md`'s symbol table defined `V̇_F` as "Fresh gas flow, L gas/min" and said no more. On an anesthesia machine that phrase has two referents that differ by a known factor:

- the **flowmeter setting**, which is carrier gas alone; and
- the flow at the **common gas outlet**, which is carrier gas plus the vapour the vaporizer added.

The circuit balance already forces the second reading. It carries one `V̇_F` for both the agent arriving and the gas leaving, so agent enters at `V̇_F·F_D` — which has to be the vapour flow the vaporizer actually adds — and mixed circuit gas leaves at that same total volumetric rate. Under a carrier-only reading the balance does not close. But the document never said which reading it meant, and the interface control a user maps onto a flowmeter bank is the one that sets it.

## What changed

**`docs/MODEL.md` § "Symbols"** — the `V̇_F` row now says "at the common gas outlet: carrier gas plus the vapour the vaporizer added, not the flowmeter setting".

**`docs/MODEL.md` § "Governing equations" → "Breathing circuit"** — two paragraphs and one display equation under the existing text: why the balance forces the total-flow reading, the derivation of `V̇_F = V̇_c/(1 − F_D)` from what a vaporizer does to the carrier stream, and the magnitude. Negligible at ordinary dial settings; 22% at desflurane's 18% Tec 6 maximum, where flowmeters set to 2 L/min leave the common gas outlet at about 2.44 L/min. That factor lands on the circuit time constant `V_C/V̇_F`, which is what the wash-in curve is about.

The three figures carry `provenance:` and `derived:` markers against `data/agents/desflurane.json`, so `doc_check` holds them to the `max_delivered_concentration_percent` they were computed from rather than leaving them as loose prose.

**`src/anesthesia_sim/core/circuit.py`** — `BreathingCircuit`'s class docstring states the same definition for `fresh_gas_flow_l_min`, since that is the one field `advance_fresh_gas()` reads as both the arrival rate and the exhaust rate; `time_constant_s` says it is a time constant on common-gas-outlet flow.

No equation, constant, parameter or test changes. The model was self-consistent under the reading its own mass balance forces; this records which reading that is.
