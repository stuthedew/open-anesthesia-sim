Closes `PL-W21J` (give the elimination comparison a test-only open-circuit driver, so washout can be validated rather than only measured), a Gate 1 P1 science entry, and unblocks `PL-73G7` (explain desflurane's opposite-sign washout), which `docket check` now reports as ready to promote.

## What the comparison could not say before

`tests/reference/test_published_wash_in.py` compared five minutes of elimination against Yasuda et al.'s four cohorts only in the shipped rebreathing condition. There the inspired fraction settles near a third of the alveolar one, and `F_A/F_A0` has no denominator term to divide it out, so agent returning from the circuit was counted as though it had come back out of the patient. Every cohort was missed by +1.1 to +5.1 published SD, and the module could state what it measured but not what the model's tissue return was worth.

## The driver

`_eliminate_without_rebreathing()` runs the same protocol with the circuit discarded after every step and the discarded agent recorded as exhausted — which is what collecting the whole expirate does, and what keeps the model's own conservation identity exact. The wash-in limb, the equations, the propagator, the step and the operating point are all the shipped ones; only the apparatus changes.

It is an open circuit to within one step: `F_I` refills to at most `V̇_A·Δt/V_C` of `F_A`, measured at 1.1e-3 against the 0.30 to 0.32 the same run settles at with rebreathing, and first-order in the step (1.111e-3, 1.111e-4, 5.555e-5 at 0.1, 0.01, 0.005 s). Sevoflurane's ratio moves 0.004 published SD between the shipped step and a step twenty times shorter, so the numbers are the `F_I = 0` limit to about three parts in ten thousand.

Per the item's brief, this is deliberately **not** a supported non-rebreathing mode: it validates a condition the shipped simulator cannot be put in, and the module docstring, every new test's docstring and `docs/MODEL.md` all say so in the same breath as the number.

## What it found

| Agent | Cohort | Shipped | Open circuit | Published |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sevoflurane | n=7 | +3.79 SD | 0.1541, −0.15 SD | 0.157 ± 0.020 |
| Isoflurane | n=7 | +4.15 SD | 0.2388, +0.66 SD | 0.223 ± 0.024 |
| Desflurane | n=8 | +1.13 SD | 0.0935, −2.33 SD | 0.14 ± 0.02 |
| Isoflurane | n=8 | +5.13 SD | 0.2388, +0.94 SD | 0.22 ± 0.02 |

Three of the four cohorts land inside the published spread once the apparatus is gone — so this model's vessel-rich return does reproduce a human elimination once the breathing systems are matched, which nothing here could say before. Desflurane over-corrects: it crosses its published mean and settles 2.33 SD below, washing out *faster* than the volunteers did. That is `PL-73G7`'s to explain.
