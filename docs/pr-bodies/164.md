Closes `PL-NSN9` (a milestone that gates itself reports `implement` when its gate clears, not `release`), entry 35 of 38 on v0.2.8's frozen list.

## The defect

`wave()` decided that a clear gate left a release to cut by `step.version < gate.milestone.version`. That is false when a milestone records its own gate, so the RELEASE branch was missed and IMPLEMENT taken. v0.2.8 is exactly that shape — `ROADMAP.md`'s own words: its frozen list "is its own scope, recorded under a gate heading because that subsection is what `bin/docket wave` reads" — so clearing the list *finishes* the milestone, and the beat would have said `implement v0.2.8 — the workflow works` when the act due was to cut the release. It surfaces once per self-gating release, at the end of it.

The same defect withheld the release from the other side. `release_offer` reads `plan.beat == RELEASE`; with the beat stuck on `implement`, a cleared v0.2.8 fell to the `RESERVED` branch — "the plan has already given this number to a step it has not finished" — and reported nothing to offer for the very release that was due. One change fixes both.

## The change

The test moves out of `wave()` into `_shipping_the_gate`, which answers yes in two arrangements rather than one:

- the step is an **earlier** milestone than the section recording the gate, so the gate earns a version of its own — Gate 0's exception, where the list frozen under v0.4.0 ships as v0.3.0. Unchanged;
- the step **is** that section, and the section records no `Required scope` of its own, so its frozen list is the whole of its content.

Widening the comparison to `<=` would not do. A milestone may record a gate *and* a scope of its own — v0.4.0 does — and standing on one of those with its gate clear, the act due is to implement the scope, not to cut a release. Version order cannot separate the two cases, so the second arrangement asks `MilestoneSection.records_its_own_scope` instead: a structural test for that one subsection, added beside `is_scoped`, which asks for all four.

## What it does not change

`test_an_open_gate_is_the_beat_whatever_else_is_written` and `test_a_clear_gate_below_the_milestone_that_recorded_it_is_a_release` are untouched and still pass. An open gate is still the beat unconditionally, and a gate still clears before the milestone it gates. Nothing about what Gate 0 binds moves.

## Tests

Two new cases stand on the same row of the same timeline with the same gate clear, and differ only in whether that milestone records a scope of its own:

- `test_a_self_gating_milestone_is_a_release_once_its_gate_clears` → RELEASE. Fails on the pre-change tree, reporting `implement`.
- `test_a_self_gating_milestone_with_scope_of_its_own_is_still_implementation` → IMPLEMENT. Passes on both, which is what it is for.
