## What it is for

**The curve the uptake literature is taught from, and the render path that can carry it.**

`PL-ZRSP` gives F_A/F_I a plot of its own beneath the compartment chart — the ratio that makes three agents comparable, the one every wash-in figure a reader has seen is drawn as, and the quantity this model's published validation is already stated in, so the trace and the validation are the same number. Most of the work is in what it refuses to draw: one series per *contiguous* stretch inside the ratio's domain rather than one polyline across the gaps, since a single line would assert a transition the model never produced; a terminal dot where a stretch stops by crossing equilibrium, because a line that simply stops is indistinguishable from one the frame clipped; and a count in words for what the segment pool cannot show.

`PL-0VM7` and `PL-MJ7B` are what make it affordable. `snapshot()` copied every sample the run had ever recorded on every render frame — 4.08 ms at half a million samples, five times a second — and the chart discarded all but the visible few hundred. The controller now answers `history_window(start_s)` with the samples at or after the axis the caller is about to draw: one cut, where the chart actually draws, instead of two that could disagree. The envelope scan reads each sample once where it read about 2.8 times.

`PL-0XMD` and `PL-5DN3` move the literature-route rule from `docs/worker.md`, which only a delegated worker reads, to a path-scoped rule that loads when a session opens the files where provenance is written. `PL-YHD3` gives two sessions on one item a computed verdict for which yields, rather than opposite answers reasoned out from one set of facts.

## Safety-relevant

**The ratio is the wash-in curve only while the dial is held.** Move the vaporizer mid-run and F_A/F_I stays a well-defined ratio of two modelled states but is no longer the textbook curve, and a learner who misses that reads a dial change as uptake. Three things carry it: v0.3.7's control-input marks appear on this plot too, the axis says the ratio is against modelled **inspired** rather than the vaporizer setting, and `docs/MODEL.md` § "F_A/F_I as a displayed ratio" states the caveat where the quantity is specified.

**Splitting one read into two moved a presentation guarantee from structure onto ordering, and the release does not leave that implicit.** The right-hand end of every trace being the same sample the readouts were formatted from held because one snapshot carried both; it now holds because `_refresh_view` is synchronous, and a test asserts it directly. Both plots are drawn from a single window read for the same reason — identical axis numbers in one frame is a weaker claim than identical samples.

**No equation, parameter, numerical method or solver step moved.** F_A/F_I is a quotient of two modelled states `core/` already held, computed outside it; the window change alters which recorded samples cross into the interface and never what any of them is.
