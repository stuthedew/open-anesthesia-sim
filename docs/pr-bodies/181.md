## What was wrong with the criterion

`PL-SZ56` pre-registers five readouts for the v0.3.0 loop trial. Readout 4 was
frozen as:

> `bin/docket stranded` reports **0 at every check** during v0.3.0.

That cannot discriminate. Within the hour of the baseline being frozen, a
second session had an open triage branch, and the original wording scored that
a failure of the branch-and-merge path. It was not one.

`bin/docket stranded` says so in its own output, and `bin/docket flight`
spells it out: *"a live session and a branch nobody will merge look the same
here; the age is what separates them."* The failure being measured is a branch
nobody will merge — which is what the three items recovered on the morning of
2026-09-02 were sitting on — not a branch a session is still working.

An instrument that fires on ordinary operation measures nothing.

## The amendment

> *Threshold (amended 2026-09-02, before v0.3.0's first entry):* zero items
> stranded on a branch with **no open pull request and no commit in the last
> 48 hours**.

This **narrows** what counts rather than relaxing it: an abandoned branch still
scores, and the original's false positives no longer do.

## Why it is an amendment and not a retrofit

The item records the date, the reason, and the fact that it was made **before
v0.3.0's first entry started**. A criterion changed after seeing the outcome is
exactly the retrofit `PL-SZ56` exists to prevent, so the note also states that
any further amendment gets the same treatment or it is not one.
