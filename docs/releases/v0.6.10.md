### defect

- PL-3B34 A dial moved on a halted run, whether failed or stopped at the 24-hour supported run length, is applied to the core and recorded as a control change at the halt instant, so the settings readouts and the control-change record show a setting that no step ever ran under beside concentrations computed under the previous one, while the banner says the values shown are the last completed step — #1846

- PL-J5GC The run screen's widgets each have one writer since PL-X7D5 and PL-WM5P, but nothing requires values shown together to come from one state, so a label, divisor or verdict can come from a different state than the value beside it: three members filed after PL-WM5P closed — #1846
- PL-HF4B After an agent-accounting failure halts a run, the rollback restores the previous step's passing validator, so the accounting panel reads "Valid" and "The simulation still accounts for all delivered agent" directly under a banner saying accounting validation failed, and the banner quotes the failed step's delivered and stored totals beside the panel's different restored ones — #1846
