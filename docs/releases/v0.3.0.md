## Loop trial

v0.2.8's claim was not that the tooling exists but that *the development loop
is reliable enough to run two long scientific milestones through*. `PL-SZ56`
pre-registered five readouts, their t=0 values and their thresholds on
2026-09-02, before v0.3.0's first entry started, so that this section compares
against what was written then rather than against what turned out to be
flattering. Scored at the release commit; window `v0.2.8`..`v0.3.0`.

| # | Readout | t=0 | v0.3.0 | Threshold | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Product vs apparatus churn | apparatus 6.63x product | product 2.08x apparatus | apparatus <= product | Pass |
| 2 | Net queue change | +37 | +19 (55 in, 36 closed) | <= +20 | Pass |
| 3 | Escaped defects | not computed | 0 confirmed, 1 contested | 0 against a safety entry | Pass |
| 4 | Stranded items | 0 | 0 excluding live branches | 0 with no open PR and no commit in 48 h | Pass |
| 5 | Digest self-reported blindness | firing every session | not firing | line stops, or `PL-K2ZK` closed | Pass |

**Readout 1 carries the most weight, and it reversed.** Across its own window
v0.2.8 moved 1,427 lines under `src/` and `tests/` against 9,463 under
`.claude/`, `tools/`, `subprojects/`, `bin/` and `Makefile` — it would have
failed this readout 6.63x over. v0.3.0 moves 4,623 product lines against 2,221
apparatus lines. `PL-C8MV` records that the partition credits apparatus tests
to product; reassigning `tests/unit/test_contrast_check.py`,
`tests/unit/test_doc_check.py` and `tests/unit/test_docket_digest_hook.py`
(483 lines) gives 4,140 against 2,704, or 1.53x. Both scorings are given here
rather than amending the readout after the outcome was known, which is the
disposition `PL-C8MV` recommends.

**Readout 2 passes with one to spare, and its baseline does not reproduce.**
Intake 55, closures 36, net +19; the open count moved 83 to 102, which is the
same +19 by an independent route. The window ends at the commit that cut the
release, so the item this close-out itself files (`PL-NG3G`, build
`bin/docket vitals`) falls outside it by construction; counting it gives +20,
which is inside the band as well, and both figures are given rather than only
the flattering one. The pre-registered confound stands and is
not waived: v0.2.8's +37 included a one-off repository-wide review that filed
65 items on 2026-08-30, so a lower number was partly expected and this is not
claimed as an improvement. A second confound was found while scoring and is
filed as `PL-40PL`: readout 2 names no command, unlike readout 1, and its t=0
figures of 141 in and 104 closed reproduce under no method tried — the
`added:`/`closed:` field dates give 138/102 and a store delta between the two
tags gives 94/78. The v0.3.0 answer is stable across all three methods (+15 to
+19, inside the band each time), so this changes no verdict; it bounds how
strongly the t=0 comparison may be stated. The `safety`/`science` subset was 5
filed and 2 still open, against 9 filed and 5 open at t=0.

**Readout 3 passes, and the one contested hit is recorded rather than
dropped.** Twenty-one `defect`-classed items were filed in the window. Under
the causal rule as amended on 2026-09-02, none is caused by `PL-026`,
`PL-0MLQ` or `PL-NV9W`. The contested case is `PL-GW37`, which names `PL-026`:
clause 1 does not fire, because its brief attributes the fault to
`_merges_naming`'s recency rule and to `CLAUDE.md`'s singular-id wording, both
older than `PL-026`; clause 2 fires on a literal reading, because the defect
reproduces at `#204` and not at its parent — `#204` created the first rider
closure. It is scored not-an-escape here, on the ground that `#204`
instantiated an older fault rather than shipping wrong work. That call is
stated rather than buried because readout 3's amendment was itself made after
seven of the nine open entries had closed, and a reader may re-score from the
ids given.

**Readout 4's zero excludes a live branch, as its amended threshold requires.**
`bin/docket stranded` reports `PL-Q8QX` on
`origin/claude/breathing-circuit-default-tckz8n` — the release branch itself,
carrying a commit minutes old and an open pull request, which the amended
threshold excludes expressly. `PL-21GS` records the confound that matters
more: every reading up to and including t=0 was taken in a shallow container,
where `stranded` is bounded by the refs the checkout holds. Landing `PL-K2ZK`
moved that from 6 refs to 7 and surfaced `PL-8DJ7` immediately. The earlier
zeros are therefore `0 of what could be seen`, and the post-deepening readings
are the stronger ones; five identical zeros are not five identical
measurements.

**Readout 5 passes on both limbs.** The digest's "N refs could not be compared
with `origin/main`" line does not fire, and `PL-K2ZK` closed in v0.2.12 via
`#214` with its reason recorded.

**Two confounds pre-registered at t=0, both still standing.** `PL-L9JS` (eight
open items carry a `verify:` command that passes without their work) is itself
a Gate 0 entry, so the trial repaired one of its own instruments mid-run, and
verify-command reliability at close-out is not measuring what it measured at
the start. And `bin/docket check` reported four grooming advisories at t=0,
one of them that `PL-0MLQ` — the entry `next` offered first — carried a
`verify:` command selecting no test. The instruments were partly miscalibrated
at t=0 and this section does not read their output as clean.

**Did the claim hold?** Yes, on the evidence available, and with the
confidence spread unevenly across the five. The loop closed 21 of 21 frozen
gate entries across the v0.2.6 to v0.3.0 series, product work outweighed
apparatus work for the first time in the project's recorded history, intake
converged inside its band, nothing escaped the gates onto a safety-classed
entry, and the two instruments that were declining to answer at t=0 now
answer. Readouts 1 and 5 are strong. Readout 2 passes under every counting
method but against a baseline that will not reproduce. Readout 4 is a genuine
zero taken on an instrument that was partly blind for most of the run.
Readout 3 passes under a rule amended after most of the entries had closed,
and has one hit that a different reading would count. The loop is reliable
enough to run v0.4.0 and v0.5.0 through, which is what the claim asserted.

**What to change before v0.4.0.** The trial's own instruments needed repair
three times while it ran — `PL-C8MV`, `PL-21GS` and `PL-40PL` all came out of
measuring rather than out of the work — which is the ordinary cost of a first
run and the argument for not scoring the second one by hand. `PL-SZ56`
deliberately did not build `bin/docket vitals`, on the ground that the trial
was the evidence for whether the numbers were worth having rather than a
prediction to act on. They were. Readouts 1, 4 and 5 are already one command
each; readout 2 needs its counting rule written down, which `PL-40PL` has to
answer regardless; readout 3 needs the causal rule frozen before v0.4.0 opens
rather than amended while it runs. Gates 1, 2 and 3 each want these same five
readouts, so the rule is settled once and read three times.
