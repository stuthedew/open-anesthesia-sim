**Problem.** PL-6ZQY's prose-asserts-facts cluster has no causing item: PL-036 records the general case as settled human judgment, so fifteen open items have no head to be marked against

**Why it matters.** The project's authoritative documents were written as prose
for a human reader and only later became the specification the code is judged
against, so the link from a sentence to the identifier, test, constant or count
it names exists only in the reader's head. `CLAUDE.md` calls a stale statement
about what a value means a safety issue rather than tidiness, and this is the
cluster that produces them.

**Why no existing item can head it - and this one is a refusal rather than a
gap.** `PL-036` is the only open item whose subject is the mechanism, and its
own `Decided` section closes the general case: *"Recorded, and not to be
revisited by tooling: whether a documented statement is still true stays
human."* Its `Done when` implements one link - fifteen bullets under
`docs/MODEL.md` "Minimum displayed outputs" named against `SimulationSnapshot`
fields. Writing `root-cause-of:` onto it would record a claim its own brief
contradicts and promote a narrow item above every band but `P0`, where nobody
could work the cluster from it. `PL-9LXK` carries the owner's decision for the
*count* half only.

**So the head is a decision about scope, not about truth.** `PL-036`'s recorded
principle - the tool checks **linkage, not truth** - is not in question. What
is unsettled is whether the *convention* is adopted: that every assertion in an
authoritative document names the tree entity it asserts, so `doc_check` can
resolve the link without deciding the sentence. That is the annotation pass
`PL-036` itself calls "eighteen judgments, not the forty lines of checking",
and it is what would close the cluster rather than one bullet list of it.

**Candidate members (15, unverified).** `PL-8LDF`, `PL-2M9N`, `PL-41YP`,
`PL-GQWP`, `PL-5N7T`, `PL-9LXK`, `PL-T9XJ`, `PL-4RHP`, `PL-DHJ7`, `PL-B8V1`,
`PL-NLP4`, `PL-LM8P`, `PL-NWTM`, `PL-B5LB`, `PL-GTSL`. The last four of those
are one-off repairs of one stale sentence each, which a head would re-point
rather than close; `PL-6ZQY` lists `PL-LM8P` as a drop candidate.

**Done when.** Either `PL-036`'s `Done when` is widened to the convention and
it carries the `root-cause-of:`, or a separate item poses that decision and
carries it, and the members are re-pointed or dropped against whichever.

**Where this came from.** `PL-6ZQY` found six clusters under one mechanism -
*the apparatus infers a fact it could have recorded* - and `PL-VX5H` built the
way to rank one: `root-cause-of:` on the item that causes the cluster, which
`docket next` then offers above every band but `P0`. Marking the six on
2026-09-17 found that only two have a causing item in the store. `PL-BHVM` and
`PL-L4YG` carry the field now; this cluster is one of the four that has nobody
to carry it, so it stays flat in `P2` exactly as `PL-VX5H` says a generator
does.

**How the membership below was arrived at, and what it is worth.** A 2026-09-17
pass read every open item's front matter, and each candidate's title and one
quoted sentence from its brief. That is enough to say these are plausibly one
problem and **not** enough to write the `root-cause-of:` line: the field is a
recorded fact rather than an inference, which is the whole of why it outranks a
`safety`-classed `P1`. Confirm each against its brief before writing it. This
is `PL-6ZQY`'s own caveat about its map, arriving at the next step.
