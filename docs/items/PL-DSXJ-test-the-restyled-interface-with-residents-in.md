**Problem.** Test the restyled interface with residents in rounds of five, scored by tasks completed and values misread

**Where this comes from.** The fifth step of the order of work the owner
agreed on 2026-11-10 (`PL-HS9M`). It waits for the token and component set,
`PL-MF90`.

**What to do.** Test with residents in rounds of five, at least two rounds,
on the four tasks in `PL-8Q1P`. Score each round by the tasks completed and
the values misread. Nielsen's page, re-read on 2026-11-10, gives the reason
for small rounds: a single user finds 31% of problems on average, a first
study with five finds 85%, and "3 studies with 5 users each" improve a
design more than one study of 15.
https://www.nngroup.com/articles/why-you-only-need-to-test-with-5-users/
The owner recruits the residents.
