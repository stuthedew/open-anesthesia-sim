**Problem.** A window shorter than the dashboard scrolls the whole page, Areas and all (`SimulationView._page_scroll`). The page can then carry the top of an Area past the window's top edge while the lower part of its View stays in view. Two things go with it. The Area's header names the View and, while a Workspace pins a run, that run (`AreaFrame.pin_label`). The row a View keeps above its scroll box (`pinned_row`, `PL-26BZ`) holds the compartment schematic's "Model outputs — not measurements.", the tensions' unit and the amounts' unit with its 20 °C and 1 atm, and the wash-in plot's statement that it is modelled. So a user can see a tension or an amount with no unit, or a wash-in trace with no statement that it is modelled, on screen.

**Why it matters.** A tension or an amount read without the line that states its unit, or an amount without the 20 °C and 1 atm it is stated at, can be read in the wrong unit or taken as stated at body conditions. A trace read without the line saying that it is modelled can be taken for a measurement. `PL-3NGZ` made each Area keep these lines at the top of the part in view, following NUREG-0700 Rev. 3 guideline 1.5-15 as the Direction paragraph below quotes it. The page's own scroll is the one box where that rule does not yet hold.

**What was measured** (`PL-3NGZ`, 2026-11-27, offscreen platform, the container's font). With the schematic as the only Area of a window 1400 by 400, two runs open, the page scrolls 182 px, and the schematic's Area starts 27 px from the page's top. Past about 71 px of page scroll the pinned row is above the window's edge while label lines are in view. `PL-3NGZ` made each run's heading follow a page scroll, so the run's name and the compartments' names stay in view; only the header and the pinned row are left. In the shipped Workspaces at 1366 by 768 and larger, the schematic's Area sits low enough that the page never scrolls its top out of view, so this needs a small window or a Workspace the user arranged.

**A second instance: a sideways scroll of the page** (the review of `PL-3NGZ`
after it merged, 2026-11-27, offscreen platform). `pin_heading` moves a run's
heading up and down only. With two runs and a branch, a window 1280 px wide,
and the schematic swapped into the Maintenance Workspace's left Area, the page
scrolls 36 px sideways. At its end the first 22 px of "Run 2, at 10m of
simulated time" are left of the view while five of that run's six tensions
stay wholly in view. At 1000 px wide the page scrolls 126 px and hides the
first 112 px of the name, which takes "Run 2" off screen. At 1366 px the page
does not scroll sideways. So it takes, as above, a layout the user arranged and
a window smaller than the shipped sizes. The fix pins the heading's text at the
left edge of the part in view as well as at its top.

**Direction.** The same rule `PL-3NGZ` applied inside an Area: a label stays at the top edge of the part in view (NUREG-0700 Rev. 3, guideline 1.5-15, p. 1-59: "General labels and row and column labels should remain along the top (or bottom) and left (or right) edges of the display"). One route is to lay the Area's header and pinned row over the top of its scroll box, as `PinnedHeadingSection` lays a run's heading over its rows, so they slide down while the page carries the Area's top away. A heading inside the View would then have to pin below them rather than at the box's top, so `in_view` would need to leave out the part they cover. The other route is to keep the page from scrolling an Area's top out of view at all. Either changes `AreaFrame`, which the corner drags and the too-small notice also use, so it wants its own pull request and a look at the Wash-in View as well as the schematic.

**Done when** a test scrolls the page of a short window through every position, with each registered View in turn at the top of the window, and finds no value wholly in view without its Area's header and the View's pinned row in view too. The test covers every View rather than the schematic and the Wash-in View alone, so that a View given a pinned row later is checked too (triage, 2026-11-28, from the Generator check below).

**Generator check.** Re-entry of `PL-P83G` and `PL-3NGZ`: a qualifier in a different widget from the values it qualifies, inside a box that scrolls. `PL-3NGZ` closed the case inside an Area and for a run's heading on a page scroll; this is the one box left, the page itself. Three items on one mechanism; whether that makes a generator is triage's call.

**Generator check, answered at triage 2026-11-28.** This item is recorded as the head of one fact: which lines a displayed value must have in view with it, and whether every box that scrolls keeps them in view together. `PL-26BZ` (the wash-in View's "Modelled" line and its plot never on screen together inside their Area, closed 2026-11-14), `PL-P83G` (a share bar on screen while the lines naming its segments had scrolled out, closed 2026-11-27) and `PL-3NGZ` (the run's name and the units scrolled away inside the schematic's Area, closed 2026-11-27) each fixed one box. No head's `misread:` states the fact (`bin/docket generators --misread`, read 2026-11-28). The page's own scroll was found the day `PL-3NGZ` closed, so the mechanism is still producing, and the Done-when above covers every View.

**Route taken, 2026-11-28: the first of the two in the Direction.** Each Area's header, the rule under it and its View's pinned row are now the heading of a `PinnedHeadingSection` that holds the rest of the Area (`AreaFrame.chrome`, `AreaFrame.body`). While the page carries the Area's top above the window's edge, they stand at the top of the Area's part in view, over the top of the View. `in_view` now leaves out the part of a widget that any heading around it covers, so a run's heading in the schematic pins under the Area's, and `clear_of_heading` is `in_view`. Each heading's place is worked out from the ancestors' geometry, not read from where it was last moved, so the order in which headings are pinned cannot change the answer. On a sideways scroll, a heading's general label (the Area's header and pinned row, and each run's name line) starts at the left edge of the part in view, while the compartments' names stay over their containers; where the part in view is narrower than the line, the line's end is cut rather than its start. The heading draws the panel's colour only while it is away from its own space, so an Area looks as before until the page scrolls it. The dashboard's scroll to the concentration chart after a branch now stops where the chart is clear of its Area's header (`scroll_clear_into_view`), where `ensureWidgetVisible` would have put the plot's top under it.

The second route, keeping the page from scrolling an Area's top out of view, cannot hold where an Area is taller than the page's viewport: the page would have to cut the Area's foot instead, or shrink it below the least height that `PL-GWTR` and `PL-D5J4` hold it to. The route taken also matches the sources. NUREG-0700 Rev. 3 guideline 1.5-15, p. 1-59 (full text from https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/1644018, read 2026-11-28): "General labels and row and column labels should remain along the top (or bottom) and left (or right) edges of the display", and guideline 1.5-16, p. 1-60, says an overlay "should not erase the overlaid data", which a heading over rows that scroll back into view does not. Blender Manual, "Regions" (source at https://projects.blender.org/blender/blender-manual/raw/branch/main/manual/interface/window_system/regions.rst, read 2026-11-28; the rendered manual served a bot check): "A header is a small horizontal strip, which sits either at the top or bottom of an area." Apple, SwiftUI documentation, "Grouping data with lazy stack views", § "Keep important information visible" (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/grouping-data-with-lazy-stack-views.md, read 2026-11-28): with `pinnedViews`, "section headers are pinned to the top of the view as the user begins to scroll", and in a horizontal stack "headers attach to the leading edge".

**Regression test.** `tests/integration/test_area_header_page_scroll.py` puts each of the eight registered Views in turn in the top Area of a column over the parameter controls, in a window 1400 by 400 with a branch open, and scrolls the page through every position; and in the left Area of a row of four wide Views, in a window narrow enough to scroll the page at least 120 px sideways, through every sideways position at the top and foot of the page. Wherever a value of any Area is wholly in view and clear of its header, the View chooser and each line of the pinned row are in view above it, and each run's name starts in view. In view is worked out from where each widget stands, not from `in_view`. Run against the code before the fix (a worktree at `3e844964` with the new test file, `getattr(frame, "chrome", frame.header)` standing in for the new attribute, `PYTHONPATH` set to the worktree's `src`): all 16 cases failed, the first with "concentration_chart at page scroll 28: 'Concentrations' is not in view" and the schematic's with "compartment_schematic at page scroll 15 across, 0 down: 'Model outputs — not measurements. Tensions are in % of 1 atm.\nAmounts are millilitres of vapour at 20 °C and 1 atm.' is not in view".

**Found and filed.** Requiring each line to be wholly in view on a sideways scroll, rather than to start in view, fails for all eight Views where the window's right edge cuts across an Area: a long line is cut at its end. That is `PL-BQB6`.

**Documents checked**: `docs/INTERFACE.md` (the Areas section gains the requirement and a note for developers; the schematic section's two sentences on the page scroll are corrected), `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` (the `pinned_heading.py` and `area_layout.py` lines), `docs/VERIFICATION.md` (its one page-scroll sentence is about the use statement and still holds), `docs/MODEL.md` and `README.md` (nothing on Areas or scrolling).

**What the blind reviews found, and what changed** (two passes, 2026-11-28).

1. Both passes found that moving a line's start right by a margin squeezed the line below its least width, so the Area's header and a run's name were cut inside the Area, beside a control, where a cut line still reads as whole. The Area's heading now slides whole (`slides_left`), and the line's end is cut at the window's edge instead. A run's heading cannot slide whole, because its compartments' names stand over their containers, so its name line keeps the margin and the share caption beside the name gives way first: its least width is 1 px, and it is not drawn while it is cut (`RunSchematic.share_text_whole`). The test now requires each qualifier at its least width, the run's name whole wherever the part of its run in view holds it beside the caption's least width and the gap, and a cut caption blank.
2. The second pass found that Tab and Shift+Tab could leave the focused control under the Area's header. Qt's scroll box stops 50 px inside its edge, and a header and a two-line pinned row are taller than that. The page and each View's scroll box are now a `HeadingScroll`, which keeps the 50 px below the headings, or as much of it as the box has room for. A new test moves the focus back through every control with Shift+Tab, from the foot of a scrolled page, and requires each focused control in an Area to be in view and clear of its header. WCAG 2.2 success criterion 2.4.11 (https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-not-obscured-minimum.html, read 2026-11-28) asks that a control taking the focus is "not entirely hidden due to author-created content", and names sticky headers as the usual cause.
3. Writing that test found a defect that was there before this item. Where one scroll box holds another, Qt asks the outer box to scroll first, to where the control stood before the inner box moved it, so the control could end up outside the part of the inner box in view. Run against the code before the fix (a worktree at `3e844964` with the new test file, `getattr(frame, "body", frame)` and `getattr(holder, "chrome", holder.header)` standing in for the new attributes, `PYTHONPATH` set to the worktree's `src`), the Shift+Tab test failed for two Views: "bookmarks: QPushButton 'Add, MAC target' is not in view" and "agent_cost: QPushButton '' is not in view" (the Set price button, named by its text). The outermost `HeadingScroll` now scrolls every box around the control, from the innermost out. The six other Views passed there, because the old header never stood over the values, so that test cannot show the header case failing before the fix.
4. Both passes noted the throwaway Mac workflow, which is removed before merge.
