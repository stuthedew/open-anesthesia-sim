**Problem.** `wash_in_stretches` (`src/anesthesia_sim/app/chart_frame.py`) extends a stretch by one crossing column only if that column's ratio is at most `WASH_IN_TERMINUS_CEILING = 1.05`. The ceiling was justified by the 1.00235 that one 0.1 s step reaches. Drawn columns are now spaced by the time base, up to minutes apart, so the first column past equilibrium is often well above 1.05. Reproduced on main: 60 min at the default dial, then dial 0 for 20 min. Under "Fit run" (2 h) the stretch ends at 0.98 with no terminus; on the 4 h base it ends at 0.87 with no terminus. After 10 min of wash-in, the 4 h base ends at 0.97 with no terminus.

**Why it matters.** A learner sees the curve stop short of the equilibrium line. That looks like a cut-off trace rather than a finished wash-in. The docstring now admits that columns can land on a later state, but the behaviour is unchanged. The only Qt test checks the 5-minute-after case that ends with a terminus. A fix could interpolate the crossing onto the line, or mark the ending some other way.

**Done when.** At every time base, a wash-in stretch that reached equilibrium ends on the equilibrium line or with a mark that says it ended there, never in clear space below it. `tests/unit/test_chart_frame.py` has `test_a_wash_in_stretch_at_a_wide_time_base_ends_at_equilibrium`, which reproduces the 60-minute run on the 4 h base.

**Checked 2026-11-22** against `d4e8200`: still applies in the code. `WASH_IN_TERMINUS_CEILING` is still 1.05 in `src/anesthesia_sim/app/chart_frame.py`. `wash_in_stretches` still leaves out a crossing column above the ceiling, so the stretch ends where it would without one. **Done 2026-11-23.** The three examples were run again on main first. At 900 px they no longer end below the line, but the same run fails at wide time bases and narrow plots: 17 of 66 pairs of time base and plot width from 300 px to 1 600 px, and 23 of 162 stretches in a wider sweep. The fix draws the stored second at which the ratio crosses equilibrium, so the stretch's last point is a real state on the far side of the line. Nothing is interpolated. `chart_time_base.wash_in_crossing_seconds` chooses those seconds and `controller._with_extremes` adds them to each drawn window and to a preview's first hour. After the fix, no pair and no stretch in either sweep ends below the line. `test_a_wash_in_stretch_at_a_wide_time_base_ends_at_equilibrium` reproduces the brief's run on the 4 h and 12 h bases, and fails without the controller change. Documents checked: `docs/INTERFACE.md` (§ "What the chart draws", updated), `docs/MODEL.md`, `docs/VERIFICATION.md` and `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` (neither describes the drawn columns' choice), and the docstrings of `wash_in_stretches` and `_with_extremes` (updated).

The blind review found two gaps, both fixed in this pull request. Past its first hour a preview is predicted only at the grid seconds, so a crossing there was never searched. `controller._with_later_crossings` now predicts each second of a pair of columns on either side of equilibrium and adds the crossing second. A search of 168 previews over the four controls found one such crossing, about two and a half hours ahead with the fresh gas at 1 L/min, and `test_a_preview_past_its_first_hour_draws_the_second_its_ratio_crosses_equilibrium` reproduces it and fails without the change. The documents also said, with no direction, that one step carries the ratio at most 3% past equilibrium. That holds only when the ratio leaves the domain, so `docs/INTERFACE.md` and the docstring of `wash_in_crossing_seconds` now say so, and say that a large rise of the dial can start a stretch below the line.

The self-audit's mutation run found twelve survivors in the new preview code. Five came from checks that `wash_in_crossing_seconds` already makes, a minimum gap between columns and a bound that keeps the two columns out of the result, and both checks were removed. Two came from reading the state at the end of the exact hour twice, which is now one name used by both calls, so the existing test of the later columns catches a wrong index. `test_a_later_crossing_is_found_between_columns_two_seconds_apart_and_never_on_one` covers the gap of two seconds, and `test_a_preview_draws_a_crossing_between_its_exact_hour_and_its_first_later_column` the pair that joins the exact hour to the later columns, which a second audit found untested. The four left are equivalent. Three change `strict=True` on a zip whose two sequences always have the same length. One negates `is_wash_in` inside a predicate that is only compared with itself at the two columns of a pair, so it changes the result only where one column has no ratio.
